## Acquisition decision and costs

Recommend **no paid acquisition and no automatic second experiment**. The immediate missing item is a justified after-availability effect large enough for the executable contract, not merely another dataset.

Binance archives are free for the permitted non-production personal research described in the [dataset terms](https://github.com/binance/binance-public-data/blob/master/TERMS_AND_CONDITIONS.md); those terms do not grant live proprietary trading rights. Historical receipt-aware crypto data are an identifiable alternative: [Tardis](https://tardis.dev/) lists Perpetuals Solo at USD 700/month and Spot Solo at USD 900/month. These are published plan prices, not a quote for the March period, tax-inclusive totals, or proof of eligibility/history entitlement. Do not subscribe: better timestamps do not cure the observed effect/cost mismatch. A paid order would require explicit owner approval of vendor, period, schema, license, billing term and total cost.

NOAA forecast archives and EIA reports have zero quoted data-access fee; compute, transfer and any broker historical-data charges are not priced here. A possible owner-selected next source-validation task is NatGas forecast-revision timing plus matched broker BID/ASK coverage. It would first need historical first-publication/receipt evidence, both forecast vintages for identical valid times, fixed demand weights, executable quote paths and contemporaneous terms. This is an unqualified option, not an enrolled hypothesis or an automatic authorization to buy weather analytics. Exact broker history price is unknown; no claim of zero all-in cost. SEC/CFTC public sources similarly do not eliminate implementation and historical provenance costs. Auction, consensus and point-in-time fund-flow packages require scoped vendor quotations; no reliable exact total was obtained.

## Falsification and the conditional strategy boundary

For any later candidate, gross conditional expected P&L must exceed spread + commissions + conversion + slippage + actual financing at its minimum ticket. Aggregate stressed loss must fit EUR 2, margin EUR 80 and reserve EUR 120. Stops do not guarantee a maximum realized loss. Missing prices, latency or fees stay missing, never zero.

The necessary rejection conditions are explicit: no authentic pre-decision variable; no increment over the preserved price/activity information set; nonpositive NET versus cash and the matched-entry baseline under frozen costs; or inability to satisfy the capital/risk/margin conditions. A pilot would additionally freeze opportunity definition, entry lag, direction, abstention, size, adverse-risk allowance and fixed/dynamic exits before labels. It would report complete consecutive ISO UTC weeks, signal maturation, MAE/MFE, premature exits, returned profit, missingness and independent event counts. Repeated bars, overlapping horizons and correlated venues do not count as independent trades. Exposed history remains DEVELOPMENT; multiplicity is not reset. No such pilot was justified here, so no new NET, exit comparison or maturity measurements exist.

HARD21 remains unchanged for the final portfolio. Even idealized serial holding gives at most 42/21/14 positions per 168-hour week at 4/8/12h, before sessions, abstention and independence. Weekly releases and one daily auction cannot alone meet 21 under one entry per event. These are necessary capacity limits, not achieved opportunities, and do not prove a project-wide structural impossibility.
