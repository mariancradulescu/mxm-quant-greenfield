Why this class: circular/moving block bootstrap is natural for stationary homogeneous score paths but resampling positions can scramble a deterministic variance profile and complicate fixed irregular masks. Stationary bootstrap adds random block-length geometry and stationarity assumptions. Dependent Gaussian multipliers can preserve tapered serial covariances, but studentizing their own synthetic paths and irregular masks needs more covariance estimation machinery. Block wild bootstrap keeps observed cluster locations, masks and volatility amplitude, with one multiplier per chronological joint cluster. It is mechanically simple, translation-invariant after null centering, and computationally feasible for full partial-null certification. Using common maxT directly targets the12-leaf joint family without independent-leaf resampling or a nominal pvalue whose finite-sample validity is assumed. Single-step maxT is deliberately used rather than selecting stepdown after observing its power.

Theory: joint bootstrap consistency under sufficiently weak temporal dependence, finite moments, nondegenerate variance and suitable increasing block-length/number-of-block asymptotics supports a null-vector maximum reference. Single-step maxT across the entire null-centered12-leaf vector is conservative for subsets of true nulls when the joint approximation/subset-pivotal semantics hold. Global-null control alone is not proof of this; test three materially different partial-null configurations. Studentization is recomputed in every wild draw, which matters with few or heteroscedastic clusters. Rademacher wild signs would be exact for independent, symmetric zero-centered cluster vectors with a valid randomization formulation; this centered bootstrap-t with weakly dependent/asymmetric clusters is NOT claimed exact.
