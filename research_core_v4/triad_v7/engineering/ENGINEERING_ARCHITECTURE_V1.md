The frozen reference law is accepted. There is no statistical ARM. The candidate
keeps its RNG/path generator, score bytecode, C++ projection and Python one-sided
certificate. It prepares causal topology once, batches the three-leg relation
reductions, and reuses exactly equal coefficient tuples within a single paired
trial. Seven zero-power cells share one evaluation but retain seven output rows.
Distinct nonzero cells never reuse projections because injected impulses alter
later features. Multiprocessing can partition fixed trial indices, with one BLAS
thread per process; no concurrent threads mutate scientific worker state.

The proposed immutable shards contain 64 contiguous indices. Trial identity and
seed stay (phase, case_id, trial_index); shard ID never enters the seed. All case
indices must exist exactly once before integer reduction. A started interrupted
shard is a case failure, not a retry invitation. Only completed durable boundaries
permit continuing never-started work. The frozen single-case attempt, no-reseed,
case order, complete-case raw checkpoint and existing interpretation gates remain.
No dispatcher is authorized unless complete parity and practical feasibility pass.

Mechanical fixtures use a separate fixed engineering seed, never a certification
trial index. They measure one-day slices and compare arrays; they produce no
empirical size/power estimate, lead evidence, or complete statistical trial.
Projection speed, day-score time and constant inference costs are extrapolated
explicitly. Such measurements cannot certify full-campaign runtime, full-trial
semantic parity, durable restart or availability of sustained compute by themselves.
The acceptance decision must distinguish observed local optimization from proven
full-campaign feasibility. Failure leaves all scientific artifacts valid and stops
before an execution arm; it does not imply an information null.
