## Null-compatible persistence

At the beginning of each original calendar day, fourteen Gaussian state
coordinates follow z_d=phi z_(d-1)+sqrt(1-phi^2) eta_d, with exact stationary
initialization. This recursion advances on missing days and weekends; no
missing response is generated as observed support. State coordinates are
independent of contemporaneous factor shocks. A larger filtration may reveal
the current state at day start; validity conditional on that larger filtration
implies validity under the required smaller causal information set.

New phi values 0.80 and 0.97 have half lives about3.1 and22.8 calendar days.
They span below/above the candidate14day blocks and are not a renamed score
AR025/050 requirement. This finite envelope does not certify every persistence
or arbitrary long memory. The11 inherited intraday/covariance/tail cases are
retained as scientific stresses and supplemented by six persistent-state cases.

Persistent global magnitude is v_d=exp(.6 z_d0-.36). E[v_d^2]=1 and
Cov(v_d^2,v_(d-1)^2)=exp(1.44 phi)-1>0. Factor column weights are multiplied
byexp(.35 z_dj); their current squared sum determines asset normalization.
This gives persistent conditional variance and cross-asset covariance while
the current shocks remain centered. Predictable fixed covariance, baseline
and variance regime changes are composed prospectively in the manifest.

The normal shocks have E[e]=0. The unit-variance t5 construction is
N*sqrt(3/chi2_5), with independent N and chi-square and mean0; the centered
skew construction is (chi2_3-3)/sqrt6, also mean0. Multiplying them by current
known volatility/loadings preserves zero conditional mean. Heavy tails/skew
are not silently Gaussianized to make a score AR work.

Within a day, every asset has the same declared AR coefficient rho_d for its
own increments: r_b=rho_d r_(b-1)+sqrt(1-rho_d^2) e_b, with independently
centered future innovations conditional on all past information and current
state. At a decision q, response increments are q+2 through q+1+h/5, so
E[Y_i,h | causal past,state]=sum_(j=2)^(1+h/5) rho_d^j * r_i,q /scale_i.
This is a common own-last-change control coefficient in the declared X span.
An arbitrary additional baseline estimate in that span cancels under Q_X.
Thus E[psi | current X,D,pasttraining,retention]=0. The predeclared day changes
of rho, covariance and variance do not change this argument. The same proof
holds for every full and leaveout projection and all four horizons.

The daily score is conditionally centered. Its magnitude, conditional

score mean is inserted. No score-AR case is in the primary manifest.

## Partial nulls and power

Only nonnull-cohort OWN target assets receive causal impulses. Target assets
are disjoint across cohorts. A true-null own asset continues its unmodified
linear AR recurrence even if its relational bridges contain nonnull assets.
Consequently, conditional on all causal features, its future conditional mean
remains in X. Later impulses cannot leak into a true-null target's path through
shared factors, because their effect is added only to specified target paths.
No horizon is independently injected; overlapping horizons follow the path.

Signal size is a predeclared coefficient times the current full normalized
RD and fixed target scale at the first future increment. Seven alternatives
retain six localized +/- cohort directions and one heterogeneous +,-,+ vector.
The fixed coefficient grid0,.05,.1,.2,.3,.4,.5,.75,1 is not bps, profit or two
daily-statistic standard deviations. MDI is characterized on that grid using
simultaneous lower power bounds; failure to locate MDI means information
unresolved within this grid, never a null or economic conclusion.
