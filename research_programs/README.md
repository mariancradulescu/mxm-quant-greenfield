# General prospective programme admission

The reusable `research_core_v4/laboratory` integration joins the preserved data collectors, Discovery schemas/ledger accounting, native commission and signed FX functions, and continuous EUR200 account evaluator. It does not replace the App custody/authority plane or reopen stopped campaigns.

A Director admits a new `mxm.laboratory-admission.v1` JSON under `research_programs/admissions/`, with source module digest, authority, mode, immutable programme definitions, priorities, finite segment budget and trial budgets. Commit it prospectively. `EXECUTE_LABORATORY.json` is a versioned execution request pointing at that admission commit/path; the same workflow then continues without Work or OWNER interaction. The admission contains arbitrarily many finite programmes and conditional DAGs, not a hardcoded pair of studies. Existing programme identities are immutable after opening; material changes require new identities and budgets. New operator implementations require a versioned pinned source, not an arbitrary import or generated executable.

Read-only workers receive the existing OWNER decryption key and VIEW account credentials. They cannot publish to GitHub. A separate keyless job publishes only ciphertext and constrained public receipts atomically under an expected-parent lease. A third keyless job can dispatch exactly one continuation. Unknown acknowledgement, a pending/modified checkpoint or duplicate invocation stops without retry. No job has both the OWNER key and contents-write/actions-write permission. The App key is never supplied, and active authority remains App-only.

The initial admission is solely functional reception. It exercises synthetic branches and negative controls plus minimum authentic cache/collector integration; it is not an alpha campaign. No synthetic performance enters the economic evidence ledger or historical attempt budget.

Supported native acquisition adapters are M5, H1 and bilateral tick windows through the already authorized collector. They do not establish unavailable history, historical funding, dated commission/margin or fills. Cache gaps stay gaps; unsupported resolutions/endpoints fail closed. Current contract catalogues cannot repair historical terms.

The causal feature DSL has completed-bar lags, bounded arithmetic/comparisons, cross-instrument inputs and UTC temporal features. Source features and entry/exit quotes are bound explicitly. Native quote screening preserves missing original exits and unknown costs. Deep evaluation reuses the existing continuous account with integer volume lattice, simultaneous positions, margin, causal FX, explicit friction/financing, frozen allocator/sizing and no capital resets. Weekly/monthly reporting includes idle periods. HARD21 is evaluated from accepted entries and never repaired with extra trades. Ruin probability remains unknown without a justified stochastic model; observed-path survival is separate.

Robustness uses prospectively specified cost stresses and chronological attribution of the same bound population against cash. DEVELOPMENT re-use is never independent confirmation. No operator qualifies a cBot; separate confirmation, portfolio robustness, C# parity and authorized demo remain required after sufficient economic evidence.
