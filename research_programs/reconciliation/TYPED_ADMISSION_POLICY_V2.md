## Different units, never a reset

The preserved baseline is 21 outcome-charged V2 identities, 29 economic result
observations, and one C033 reservation without an economic outcome. The raw
legacy `used=22` remains historically true. V2 has a frozen limit of 84:
63 identities not outcome-charged, but only 62 unencumbered after the existing
hold. There is no refund, automatic hold release, or retroactive result rewrite.

Future events are separately journaled: FAMILY_REGISTERED,
PROGRAMME_REGISTERED, IDENTITY_RESERVED, ECONOMIC_OUTCOME_OPENED,
ECONOMIC_OBSERVATION_RECORDED. Registration and reservation do not themselves
claim that economic results exist. Information exposure, including outcome-free
feature diagnostics, belongs in the programme's adaptive selection history;
these counts cannot be substituted for independent hypothesis counts.

Each family and programme has a positive finite identity and observation cap.
A family's cap is shared across programme names. A distinct lane must have an
explicit finite lane cap and a scientific decision binding its novelty rationale,
selection history and adaptive exposure. V2=84 is not a project-wide cap.
Renaming cannot reset a registered lane or family. Exact semantic fingerprints
ignore identity metadata and universe ordering across lanes. This detects exact
semantic relabeling, not every statistically dependent variant: the Director
must review substantive novelty and shared multiplicity before registration.
The code's presence/length checks are not proof of scientific novelty.

## Exposure boundary and strict support gate

Reuse preserved data first. Before reserving any future identity, bind the
mechanism, causal feature clocks, quote/FX dependencies, population coverage,
dated cost authority or justified conservative bounds, EUR200 volume/margin
feasibility, finite resources, stop criteria and selection history. Never select
only profitable or conveniently supported events after outcome exposure.

The native screen hook opens an identity only when a supported native account
result can be exposed. Empty intents, missing entry/exit quotes and a missing
account result do not charge an economic identity. Any exposed gross result
does count even if costs are unresolved or the final verdict is insufficient.
Native replay conservatively opens before its first economic calculation;
subsequent insufficiency cannot erase that exposure. Observation capacity is
checked before exposure and again when recording a result. Ambiguity is a
safe-stop, never an automatic refund or blind economic retry.

New schema-v1 economic admissions fail closed in the new source. Typed
schema-v2 admissions bind the exact registered manifest and require the typed
book. Operators without exact exposure integration (generic screen/replay,
robustness and synthetic catalogue injection) remain barred for typed research.
The new admission module is included in the source-module digest.

## What this verification does not activate

The finite proof runs only new artificial accounting/unit fixtures and a read-only
comparison against checkpoint16. Unit fixtures are not market observations.
It constructs a typed baseline in memory, round-trips it, and binds its digest
to the original checkpoint. It does not replace the checkpoint, migrate source
or active authority, register a real family/programme, reserve a candidate,
acquire broker data, open economic outcomes or release the economic workflow.

Before economic execution there must be an explicit forward, encrypted,
read-back-verified successor checkpoint, preserved predecessor, correctly
bound source/admission, and durable ambiguous-exposure handling. No claim of
complete end-to-end economic readiness is made by passing these unit tests.

Current quote support (17 strict bilateral moments on six of the fixed29
sampled identities during the M5 interval) is sparse support, not causal
entry/exit/FX coverage. Current catalogue fees/margin are not dated historical
authority. Therefore no new economic programme has yet earned admission.
The next scientific decision must be based on an exact dependency and cost
join, not on the names of these six instruments, hypothetical profits or a
relaxation of C033. Closed B resources remain closed; no budget is reset here.
