It asks whether directional asymmetry of *observed quote changes*, rather than magnitude of lagged returns or traded order flow, forecasts subsequent minute reference-price movement. Microstructure information arrival is the causal rationale, not proof that historical timestamps are arrival/execution times. No global originality claim is made.

Use only the already exposed authentic EURUSD W35/W36/W37 originals (2,129,692 side events), their exact native decoder and existing custody. For each UTC minute, count +1/-1 for each BID or ASK change from its last known raw integer value; equal repeats are not changes. Normalize signed changes by number of changes, with zero for none. This is **quote-change imbalance**, not traded volume, trade initiation, order imbalance, displayed depth or liquidity. Same-ms conflicting side observations become unknown, never favorably deduplicated. Reference midpoint is last known non-crossed two-side midpoint within the completed minute, including equality; age/positive-spread executability is NOT inferred.
