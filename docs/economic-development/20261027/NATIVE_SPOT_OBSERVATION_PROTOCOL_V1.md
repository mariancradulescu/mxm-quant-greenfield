# Prospective native spot observation source pilot — 27 October 2026

## Question and selection fixed before acquisition

The exact historical GBPUSD W37 source had six observed five-minute stretches, while the three EURUSD weeks had none under the same five-second consumer guard. Neither result measures actual arrival order or firm continuous execution. This pilot asks whether the **existing authenticated native spot route** supplies both-side updates with server timestamps and measurable local receipt order/age. It also captures native contemporary symbol contracts and minimum-volume expected-margin responses for later separately admitted valuation work. It does not test a price response, direction, holding strategy, portfolio, account path, economic candidate or costs-after-profit.

Fixed panel: EURUSD/id1, GBPUSD/id2 and SpotCrude/id250, reusing identities from existing authenticated native historical/spot/DOM work. The panel contrasts the two historical source cases and one previously authenticated non-FX instrument; it is not a ranking of returns or a definition of the entire Pepperstone universe. All three are retained in the output, including zero-event and unavailable observations. Current native catalogue identity/enabled checks precede symbol-specific calls; absence/mismatch stops rather than substitutes an instrument. The 1,576-symbol metadata snapshot covers a different roster and is not used to invent these identities.

## Finite preregistered contract

One push attempt on installation of `mxm-spot-observation-reception.yml`, no dispatch, retry or reconnect. One successful inherited TLS connection, bounded original endpoint discovery, up to 480 seconds contact including authentication/metadata, then 360 seconds of observation after subscription acknowledgement. One batched spot subscription with server timestamps, no depth, history, trendbar, order, account mutation, refresh token, App or privileged writer. Existing VIEW scope, native account fingerprint, Pepperstone and EUR deposit identity are required and independently checked again from original responses. Fixed business caps: three authentication requests, one each Trader/AssetList/SymbolsList/SymbolById/Subscribe/Unsubscribe and three minimum-volume expected-margin requests. Every outbound frame has a positive narrow guard; direct/repeated business frames cannot bypass request accounting. Heartbeats reuse the existing monotonic implementation.

Caps: 100,000 market messages, 200,000 received envelopes, 16 MB framed wire including partial reads, maximum individual frame/metadata response 500 kB, 12 MB canonical market records and 24 MB total private output. Inputs beyond a cap stop collection without retry. Raw native spot envelopes, request/response correlation (excluding secret-bearing authentication requests), current terms, local UTC/monotonic clocks and exact response identities are encrypted to the existing recipient. No token, secret-bearing request, plaintext market price/event timestamp or account identifier is published.

First spot response can be a closed-market latest snapshot. It is **never** counted as freshness proof: each side must subsequently update. For a healthy received event, both sides must be positive/noncrossed/nonlocked, separately updated after initialization, at most 5,000 ms old by local receipt age and server timestamp versus local UTC, with no future/reversed server time. Server/host clock synchronization is not certified. Missing/unusable observations remain counted and break stretches. Observed spans use monotonic receipt time, with maximum five-second inter-event gaps and a fixed 300-second threshold. No interpolation before, between or after observations, or selection of the best instrument/period. This differs deliberately from historical same-server-timestamp grouping and is not retroactively applied to the old sources.

The bounded receive deadline can close a connection containing an unfinished trailing frame. Preserve that fact; never resume a partial frame, reconnect, extend the observation span to the deadline, or claim an unsubscribe acknowledgement that did not arrive. Accepted source observation with an explicitly closed tail is not a claim of complete continuous feed reception or executable liquidity. Early interruption or insufficient registered capture duration is incomplete and stops.
