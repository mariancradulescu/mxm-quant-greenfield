# PR198 / 26 October 2026 — Native cTrader Symbol fee estimates into the existing EUR entry gates

## Why
After PR196 the real C# Robot still passed `0m` as **unknown** estimated entry commission into `MxmCapitalAdmission` and the immutable EUR80/120/2 OWNER gate. This could admit a broker-request path with underestimated commission even if the native instrument advertises nonzero fees. The existing *offline* account already supports a conservative two-side calculator (PR194); no real-time broker adapter had been joined to native entry.

## New reusable native path
`MxmNativeCommission` is pure C# and compiled into the existing net6 cBot, without a parallel account engine or custody. It maps **all four** documented SymbolCommissionType values and the two documented SymbolMinCommissionType values, applies minimum fee in its declared asset, and prices both current ASK and BID at the normalized volume. The MAX current per-side estimate enters `MxmCapitalAdmission` and `MxmOwnerEntryEnvelope` as an **actual nonzero estimate**, not `0m`; two such estimates must fit inside the existing declared roundtrip friction reserve, otherwise entry HOLD. The same existing stop-risk, durable checkpoint, identity fence, broker book and capital continue to apply.

The actual Robot reads `Symbol.Commission`, `CommissionType`, `MinCommission`, `MinCommissionType`, `MinCommissionAsset`, `QuoteAsset`, `LotSize`, current BID/ASK and `AssetConverter.Convert` to EUR/USD. Undefined enums, impossible/zero/negative needed FX, bad prices, missing asset, overflow and unfunded fees lead to no order. A changed BID/ASK between estimate and last lease check leads to no new entry on that intent. Valid literal zero commission+minimum from current native properties is distinguishable from an UNKNOWN conversion; no missing source is silently charged zero. No hardcoded EURUSD convention or inferred currency from instrument name.

## Scope of evidence and limitations
These are **current API-based estimates**, not independently timestamped loss-side FX, native broker fee charge proofs, future fill-price bounds, swap, guaranteed slippage/stop loss or historic Pepperstone commission validity. `AssetConverter.Convert` does not attach separately authenticated BID/ASK source clocks; no guarantee is made that a future actual broker charge is bounded by the current calculation. Broker/order request processing remains unchanged and unactivated. To certify an all-in result, use actual callback, charge and FX evidence with independent reconciliation.

A distinct net8 synthetic fixture compares **eight type/minimum combinations** against the existing independent captured-terms calculator at both sides and checks unknown FX, negatives, stale book geometry, arithmetic overflow, exact zero fees and OWNER cash effects. Existing required CI compiles net6 cBot; no fee-price test is a real market/account economic trial. No new source request, key/environment access, simulated capital funding or economic rerun; previous Pepperstone W35–W37, all 1576 terms and existing negative/disposition capsules are unchanged. B–E, after-cost edge, HARD21 and native cTrader operational acceptance remain OPEN.
