## Opt-in route and preregistration
The existing MxmPortfolioAccountReplay gains an OFF-by-default `NativeSizingScenarioSha256`. Its values must be 64 lowercase hex characters, and EVERY received quote must carry the matching identifier and explicit broker minimum, maximum and step volumes. In the legacy null-scenario mode, extra sizing terms are prohibited; all old records/parameters maintain prior JSON shape and original fixed volume execution. No expired model is revived and no new dataset is accessed.

When enabled, the replay runs the EXACT same `MxmNativeVolumeSearch.LargestAffordable` and current account `MxmCapitalAdmission`, `MxmStopRiskAdmission` and immutable `MxmOwnerEntryEnvelope` with current one-account equity/free margin, existing stop estimate and configured fee/friction. Each candidate must fit on the explicit volume grid and existing captured-cost volume lattice when present. Fee for captured costs is recalculated for each candidate; for the general per-quote constant commission scenario, the *original* quoted fee remains fixed, never prorated down optimistically. Selected volume is written into the same position, cash, gross/fee, stop, margin, ledger, ISO week count and existing cold-capsule state. Restore demands the exact scenario identity and source SHA. There is no separate account, portfolio, batch simulator or cash reset.

**Material economic assumption:** only for this expressly labeled counterfactual, quote.InitialMarginEur and quote.EstimatedStopRiskEur are assumed linear in units from the quote's stated upper-volume baseline; actual broker tiered margin, slippage/commission and stop risk can differ. An authentic Pepperstone study cannot claim current/historical terms, fill-feasibility or native cTrader parity without separate source admission and broker evidence. This work proves compatibility of a PURE SELECTOR and cash bookkeeping on SYNTHETIC declared inputs, not an after-cost signal. No selection of economic winners or threshold retuning follows.
