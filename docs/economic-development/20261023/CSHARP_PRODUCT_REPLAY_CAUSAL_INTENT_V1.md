## Actual defect
The replay required a quote strictly after the observed bar-close signal. The real product adapter instead called `ApplyPortfolioDecision` directly from `Bars.BarClosed`, so its market order could use the same incoming tick that delivered the signal. The two executable paths did not share their causal execution policy. Neither path expired an unfilled signal at its next bar boundary. A weekend/reconnect tick can also deliver an old session's final closed bar long after it became stale.

## Implemented correction
`MxmCausalIntent` is compiled into the actual existing cTrader `.algo` and both multi-symbol replay programs. A decision is published from BarClosed and consumed only once by a strictly later symbol quote before the next bar boundary. Each symbol has its own intent, sharing the original single-account order path. A next-boundary quote cannot execute the previous signal even if the symbol Tick callback precedes BarClosed. Invalid or future clock metadata is rejected. Stop clears outstanding intents; a fresh instance does not replay them.

The product subscribes to each configured `Symbol.Tick`, validates quote shape, consumes before any broker operation, then uses the existing position, account, margin and estimated stop-risk gates. Rejected operations do not cause tick-by-tick retries of the same intent. Long-delayed closed bars clear the intent and reset the six-bar kernel instead of acting on stale prices or stopping permanently at the ordinary session gap. Subscriptions are removed on Stop. Pending *decision intents* are strictly in-memory; they are not broker pending orders, and no broker order is canceled or seized.

The replay uses the exact same intent gate. Tests exercise equal-time ticks, later quotes, independent symbols, single consumption after rejected/unknown ACK, neutral replacement, expiry before the next callback, cold start, future boundaries, delayed observation and invalid UTC. Account replay regressions require no entry at signal time, one later entry and no stale next-boundary entry. CI additionally compiles the original real cTrader binary and checks that BarClosed cannot call the trading function directly. These are offline artificial tests, not a native cTrader DEMO/Cloud session. Actual platform callback ordering and broker ACK/restart behavior remain to be demonstrated operationally.

API references used for adapter wiring: [Symbol.Tick](https://help.ctrader.com/ctrader-algo/references/MarketData/Symbols/Symbol/Tick/), [IServer.TimeInUtc](https://help.ctrader.com/ctrader-algo/references/Application/IServer/TimeInUtc/). Runtime behavior is not inferred solely from documentation.
