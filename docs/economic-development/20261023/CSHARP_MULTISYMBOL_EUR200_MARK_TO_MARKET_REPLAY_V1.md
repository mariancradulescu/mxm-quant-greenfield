# One-account multi-instrument C# economic replay — implementation / limitations

Date 2026-10-23, existing B–E development branch. This expands the *same* C# product's shared decision and account-admission research code, not a second robot or independent strategy family.

## Substantive accounting advancement
Previously, the existing native BID/ASK `EconomicReference.cs` modeled a **single** instrument and single open position, while PR #126–127 made the deployed cBot a **multi-instrument** orchestrator with a single actual broker account. It was impossible to evaluate simultaneous independent signals on one **continuous EUR200** equity/margin budget with executable-side marks.

`MxmPortfolioAccountReplay.cs` now provides a C# multi-symbol replay using the exact shared `MxmDecisionKernel`, `MxmCapitalAdmission`, `MxmStopRiskAdmission`, and `MxmAccountIsolation` contracts. The account is initialized *once*, with no per-symbol cash reset. Every new entry competes for one cash/equity/free-margin/aggregate-stop-risk budget. Longs open at ASK and mark/close at BID; shorts open at BID and mark/close at ASK. Historical conversion-to-EUR, fixed per-side commissions, price slippage, executable volume, and margin are explicit quote/event inputs; each fee is charged once, not twice. Floating PnL of all positions and their combined margin contribute to admission. Missing or stale marks on any existing symbol refuse further entries. Opposing signals close/reopen chronologically under the default `ReverseOnly` policy.

Completed-bar decisions are independent causal six-bar histories, per symbol. A bar-generated decision can only execute on a **strictly later** quote for the same symbol, never at the bar close used to generate the signal. Input is globally UTC ordered, with fail-closed unknown symbols, invalid spread, nonpositive conversion/volume/margin/risk, nonmonotonic bars or quote replay. Shared-account weekly counts are based on actual *modeled admissions* using ISO UTC week numbers, but the engine **never** asserts HARD21 for a partial window or a synthetic replay.

An executable synthetic two-instrument fixture checks EUR200 starting capital, two separately signed positions, exact single-account cash EUR199.80, liquidation-side equity EUR200.20 and EUR140 aggregate margin, cross-symbol capital cap/risk/margin suppression, no same-close synthetic fill, invalid/crossed quotes and global chronology rejection. It also explicitly demonstrates that changing the relative arrival order of same-timestamp quotes can change which trade gets the capital slot. CI checks the resulting C# account by *independent Python Decimal* arithmetic, not just self-reported C# booleans.

## Critical unproven dependencies
This is an **offline execution-sensitive counterfactual engine**, not a native cTrader fill emulator. Only default ReverseOnly exit lifecycle is simulated; real stop executions, slippage during gaps, rejected/partial fills, swaps, cross-instrument market data arrival, exchange conversion timing, per-instrument broker margin minima and future quote freshness remain unresolved. The provided quote price, `QuoteToEur`, volume lattice, commission, slippage, stop-risk and margin values must be obtained and authenticated separately for each historical instrument and time; merely populating them with synthetic or assumed numbers does **not** certify broker costs or execution. A same-timestamp ordering chosen by a serialization rule cannot establish broker arrival order. The native 2026-09-09 four-hour EURUSD-only BID/ASK capture is insufficient to populate independent multi-instrument historical streams or a complete ISO week; the historic M5 corpus lacks historical BID/ASK fill and cost authority.

Thus there is no legitimate economic selection, independent confirmation, candidate promotion, HARD21 proof, or Cloud operational certificate from this PR. The existing C033/V2 research registry, original exposed data, protected windows, OWNER1807, authority refs, GitHub rulesets, trading permissions and native cost custody are untouched. No real broker requests and no LIVE/DEMO trades occur.
